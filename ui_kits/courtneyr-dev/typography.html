Why I left the framework treadmill

Eyebrow: Roboto Slab 700 small caps. Title: Roboto Slab 800. Body: Barlow 400. The hierarchy is family + weight, never color.

hello there

Rock Salt is decorative — used once per page, sparingly, and always with a slight rotation. Pair with calm Roboto Slab so the page doesn't get noisy.

The rules

don'ts
Type rules
  • Rock Salt only at --cr-text-2xl or larger. Never below.
  • Rock Salt once per view. If you want it twice, you want one Rock Salt & one Roboto Slab 900.
  • Body type is Barlow. Headings are Roboto Slab. Don't mix.
  • Tracking is set on the eyebrow, not on body. Don't space-letter prose.
