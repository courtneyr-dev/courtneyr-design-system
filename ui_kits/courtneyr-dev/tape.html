Sparkles

.cr-sparkle · --margin

Sparkles are ✦ decorative ✺ asterisks. Inline they sit on the baseline. The --margin modifier positions them absolutely.

✺ ✦ ✦

Always aria-hidden="true" in markup — they are not content. On narrow viewports (<60rem) hide them with display: none so they don't clip into copy.

Highlight

Run any inline phrase through a highlighter swipe to give it the marker pass. Always slightly off-center (vertical 50–92%) so it feels hand-drawn.
