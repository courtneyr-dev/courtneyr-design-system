Cards & callouts

Boxed content. v1.x had three card variants (default, elevated, inverse); v2.0 collapsed to a single zine card with a -1.2° rotation and a hard 6×6 shadow. Callouts and accent blocks fill the role of the removed variants.

Card

.cr-card

Notes from Tuesday

Rotation -1.2°. Hard shadow 6×6 in ink. Sharp corners. Surface-elevated background reads slightly lifted from the ivory page.

One variant only. v1.x's .cr-card--elevated and --inverse were removed in v2.0.

Callouts

.cr-callout · --note · --warn
Default

Cerulean surface, ivory ink. The masking-tape label uses a tilted .cr-callout__label.

Note

Prussian-blue surface, light-orange ink. For asides that should sit calmer on the page.

Warn

Selective-yellow surface, prussian-blue ink. For deprecation notices, AA failures, sharp warnings.

Accent blocks

.cr-accent-block--1/2/3

Block 1 — sky

Sky-blue surface, russian-violet ink. The cool accent — used for sidebars, info panels.

Block 2 — periwinkle

Periwinkle surface, russian-violet ink. The lavender accent — feels softer, used for testimonials and quotes.

Block 3 — light orange

Light-orange surface, russian-violet ink. The warm accent — used for table-of-contents (.cr-toc inherits these tokens) and "what you get" lists.

Dark mode auto-flips: block 1 → prussian + sky text, block 2 → russian + periwinkle text, block 3 → cerulean + light-orange text. No component changes required.
