Rotation budget

four values, no more
--cr-rotate-1 · -1.2°
--cr-rotate-2 · 1.5°
--cr-rotate-neg · -2.5°
--cr-rotate-neg2 · 2.8°

Rule: never invent a fifth rotation. Body text, tables, figures, and forms stay upright — rotation is reserved for accents (tape, sparkles, marker bars, sole-card).
