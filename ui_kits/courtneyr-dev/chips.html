05 · Components

Chips

The 18-type post taxonomy as masking-tape labels (WordPress standard formats + IndieWeb extensions + Courtney's speaking/book types). Default chip = cream tape with a 4 px left-border accent in the post-type color. Solid chip = filled in the post-type color, ink adapts.

Default chips · all 18 types

.cr-chip + .cr-chip--{type}
Blog Aside Image Gallery Video Audio Chat Status Link Bookmark Quote Speaking Book Like Repost Reply Event Review

Russian-violet text on cream tape = 12.47:1 (AAA). The 4 px left border carries the post-type color so accent never compromises legibility. Every accent verified ≥3:1 vs tape (WCAG 1.4.11 non-text contrast).

Solid chips

.cr-chip + --{type} + --solid
Blog Video Audio Link Bookmark Quote Speaking Like Reply Review

Eight types do not support --solid — their accents fail AA against both ivory text and russian-violet text: aside, image, gallery, status, chat, repost, book, event. Use the default tape variant for those.

v2.1 — what changed

9 → 18
Added (9 new types)

image, gallery, chat, bookmark, like, repost, reply, event, review — bringing chips to full coverage of the WordPress standard formats + IndieWeb taxonomy.

Recolored (1 type)

blog moved from UT-orange to russian-violet (its primary brand color, freeing UT-orange for video). video moved from cerulean to UT-orange. audio moved from cerulean to selective-yellow. status moved from glaucous to blue-green. speaking moved from selective-yellow to ut-orange-dark. book moved from russian-violet to light-orange-dark.

don't bring it back
