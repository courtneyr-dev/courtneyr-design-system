03 · Brand

Layering

v1.x had floating shapes — soft circles behind portraits, badge orbits, a vocabulary called shape-layering. v2.0 deleted all of it. Depth now comes from four humble tools, every one of which already exists on a real photocopied zine.

The four tools

that's the entire toolbox
01 · hard offset shadow

Cards lift off the page through a flat 4×4 px shadow in --cr-ink. No blur, no opacity ramp.

--cr-shadow-hard  ·  4px 4px 0 var(--cr-ink). The 6×6 variant exists for cards that need more lift.

02 · rotation
field notes draft 2024

Four rotations only. -1.2°, 1.5°, -2.5°, 2.8°. Body type, tables, figures, forms stay upright.

03 · halftone

Dotted screentone behind hero/landing surfaces. Adds tactile texture without competing with type.

--cr-halftone · 12 px grid, 0.08 ink. --cr-halftone-strong · 6 px grid, 0.18 ink — for genuine screentone moments only.

04 · paper fiber

A 1px horizontal fiber at very low alpha, applied at the body level. You only see it when you look — but it's why the page feels like paper.

--cr-fiber · repeating-linear-gradient on body. Always-on. Don't disable it.
